Sensors

distance (cm)
Ultrasonic ping in centimetres. −1 means no echo (too close, too far, or aimed at empty air). HUD DIST is the same reading.
IR left / right
True when that bumper IR sees an obstacle (the pin reads LOW). False means that side is clear. Use with if or wait until.
battery (V)
Pack voltage, about 6.6–8.4 V. Not a percent.
compare
True/false test. Plug sensors or numbers into both sides. IR is 1 when blocked and 0 when clear.
threshold
Named follow/avoid distances from the sliders below. Apply settings, then compare against distance.
